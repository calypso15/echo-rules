# ECHO-10: Atacama Desert Outpost

## Location Data

- **Designation**: ECHO-10 | **Local Name**: Nueva Esperanza ("New Hope")
- **Coordinates**: 22°55'S, 68°12'W | Atacama Desert, Chile (~2,400m elevation)
- **Status**: 100% operational, undamaged
- Standard 1km hexagonal structure, 140+ sub-levels. Deepest human penetration: sub-level -127 (Guildmaster Yuki Tanaka, deepest at any site worldwide). Self-illumination below -20, no degradation since 2035.
- Environment: Near-zero precipitation, UV >15, ~76 kPa ambient, 25-35°C daily swings, cosmic radiation ~2x sea level, high-albedo surfaces (80-85%).

## Pre-Strike History

- Construction pod arrived March 15, 2035 (14:47 UTC). Emergency presidential decree invoked military administration within 4 hours; Constitutional Tribunal briefed after the fact.
- 40km radius civilian evacuation by May 2035. Indigenous Atacameño/Aymara displaced under emergency order — compensation criticized, grievances persist in Ring 5.
- CONICET-Chile + international consortium deployed ~350 researchers under dual Ministry of Science/CNI (intelligence) command. UN observers restricted to outer perimeter; reports subject to Chilean intelligence review (protested, denied).
- India-China ECHO-06 conflict (Oct-Dec 2035) sharpened Chilean strategic posture: Ring 1 fortification accelerated, no external military forces within 100m exclusion zone (maintained through 2047).
- Chile briefed as "stable allied site" during Echo Protocol planning but not in authorization chain. Chilean executive later acknowledged awareness of planned strikes; decision not to oppose was calculated.

## Post-Strike & Political Development

- Sept 9, 2036: ECHO-10 unharmed. Resonance field spiked ~18 hours (shorter than ECHO-09's 36 hours), stabilized at ~15% above baseline. Ground subsidence in exclusion zone; no casualties.
- Chile broke from NATO "Watcher self-destruct" cover story within 72 hours — earliest condemnation from an aligned nation. Strategic calculation: sole intact continental site, sovereignty benefits of early repudiation outweighed costs.
- Nueva Esperanza construction began Jan 2037 (planned for 50K; reached 500K by 2039).
- **Santiago Summit** (Sept 2037 - Mar 2038): Chile leveraged ECHO-10 proximity into favorable terms — broad Article I sovereignty language, effective veto over researcher access, 3-year exclusivity window (extended from 2 at Chilean insistence). Outpost Alliance (Chile + Republic of Maohi Nui) formalized.
- Governance consolidated under expanded executive authority framed as emergency. Constitutional suspensions remain in force (2047). Domestic opposition categorized as security risk; international criticism acknowledged and set aside.

## Structure

### Materials
- Standard alien ceramic-metal composite (no site-specific geological adaptation, unlike ECHO-09's PB-Composite). Whether this reflects continental standardization or Atacama substrate limitations is actively disputed between ECHO-09 and ECHO-10 research teams.
- Three breach events (2037 rock strike, 2039 vehicle collision, 2042 breach charge): composite self-repaired in 6-48 hours with no observable mechanism; no material recoverable.
- Highest confirmed cosmic radiation absorption of any intact site. Hypothesized as passive energy harvesting (not just shielding). Power output slightly below ECHO-09 (attributed to absence of PB-Composite thermal enhancement).

### Resonance Field
- Extends ~480m from perimeter (larger footprint than ECHO-09's 320m but lower intensity per unit area — dry high-altitude environment extends range while reducing density).
- Directional asymmetry: stronger toward south-southwest (unexplained, mapping ongoing).
- Electronics degrade within 480m, acutely within 100m exclusion zone. All Ring 1 networks on hardened fiber-optic; no classified wireless within 800m.
- Personnel health effects: Elevated oxidative stress, cortisol suppression, anomalous circadian shifts. Not formally acknowledged by Chilean health authorities; Guild medic records document consistently since 2037.
- Stable thermal anomaly at sub-levels -44 to -47: ~4°C above adjacent levels (continuous since 2040, no explanation).

## Nueva Esperanza (Pop. 2.3M)

Concentric rings surrounding ECHO-10. All rings have north + south gates (except Ring 0: south only). Gates have security checkpoints with guards, mantraps, credential scanners. Formal names by ring/direction; unofficial local names in common use.

### Ring 0: ECHO-10 Outpost
- The alien structure + 100m exclusion zone. Single entry: Gate 0 South (Chilean military + UN observers per Article II). EM interference degrades electronics; resonance field directionally non-uniform; biological effects unacknowledged. Active energy process with no human analogue.

### Ring 1: Government & Military
- Chilean military/police/government HQ, international research delegations, UN observer missions.
- All classified networks on hardened fiber-optic. Continuous ECHO-10 monitoring array; raw data withheld from Accord observers (national security; ~20% of quarterly UN reports flag this).
- Researcher selection flashpoint: China alleges bias; Heritage + US universities get ~40-45% of international slots; Crater Coalition allocations well below proportional share.

### Ring 2: Corporate
- CMC 80-story HQ (~20% of commercial real estate). Also: second-tier extraction corps, artifact IP law firms, private security, primary ₳ clearing exchange for South American markets.
- PROMETHEUS quantum computing node in seismically isolated sublevel on Watcher power (bypasses city grid).
- 12,000+ active hybrid-tech patents (substantially derived from Cobre findings).
- Highest-value square kilometer in Southern Hemisphere.

### Ring 3: Diving
- Diver economy core: outfitters, robot maintenance, charging grids.
- **Diver's Rest** (Guild HQ): Converted warehouse, inner edge. 24/7 neutral ground, info exchange, emergency coordination.
- Operator lounges vs human specialist bars (memorial walls, no-robots). Boundary dissolves after midnight.
- Guild rescue: Red (medical), Blue (extraction), Black (deep-zone), Gold (classified). 15-min avg callout.

### Ring 4: Residential & Academic (~900K pop)
- Most densely populated ring; civic/domestic heart. Housing self-segregates: diver families near Ring 3, admin/academic mid-ring, most crowded at Ring 5 boundary.
- Pressurization "basic service" — routine failures, cold-exposure/altitude illness surges. Residents maintain personal pressure monitors + shared emergency O₂.
- **Academic District** (northern quarter, Via de la Ciencia):
  - **UTNE** (est. 2040): 7 faculties (xenomaterials, resonance physics, xenolinguistics, post-Contact medicine, artifact IP law). Key pathway Ring 4 → Ring 1/2.
  - **Heritage Foundation field campus**: ~40 rotating researchers adjacent to UTNE xenolinguistics. Formally cooperative, practically strained (exclusivity disputes).
  - 3 secondary technical institutes + nursing college (all underfunded).
- **Synthesis Collective** (southern edge): ~500 members in converted water-treatment compound. Augmentation, resonance enhancement, consciousness merging. Chilean intel surveils continuously. Lilith not seen since 2045.
- **Services**: 3 hospitals (2 state, 1 Heritage), 14 clinics, resonance/altitude trauma center. 12 hydroponic farms ("adequate if monotonous").
- **Ordo Duodecim**: Hall of Twelve Voices (western sector). Services 6 days/week. Cultural institution since 2042.
- **Found Initiative**: Reintegration office for Lost with Ring 4 housing.
- **Politics**: Most organized civic energy in the city. Pure Earth cells more ideologically coherent here (academic families, resonance-damage relatives, religious opposition). Two academics assassinated (2043, 2046, unsolved). Sharpest fault line: Synthesis Collective (opposed by Ordo and Pure Earth alike).

### Ring 5: Slums (~400K-800K est.)
- Everything beyond Ring 4: farms, heavy industry, shantytowns. No entry controls, no legal protections. "Transitional informal settlement" classification for 9 years (defers obligations).
- **Crimson Dawn Syndicate** (Red Queen) = de facto government: dispute arbitration, trade taxation, minimal order.
- Population: Economic migrants, displaced indigenous Atacameño/Aymara, disproportionate Lost and families (employment discrimination channels them outward). Found Initiative largest field office here.
- ~30% of Pure Earth personnel in South America originate from Ring 5. Syndicate + Ordo cells + Pure Earth operate in close, occasionally violent proximity.
- Unpressurized (76 kPa): Chronic hypoxia, cardiovascular strain, altitude polycythemia. Children show developmental differences vs pressurized rings. "Red-blooded" reclaimed as resilience symbol. Partial pressurization estimated at <2% of Ring 2 energy — no action in 9 years.

## Ecology

- **Within 480m resonance field**: Anomalous mineral crystallization, elevated soil conductivity. No life within 200m. Sparse extremophile communities (microbial mats, lichen) in 200-480m boundary since 2041.
- **Project Altiplano zone (~60km radius)**: Soil microbiome diversification since 2044; vicuña and flamingo ranges expanding. Chile's flagship "Watcher benefit" narrative.
- **Thermal effects**: Watcher energy venting has increased nighttime temps within 30km by +1.1°C since 2039. Localized microclimate: shortened frost season, increased dew. Effects on extremophile ecology classified under Cobre exclusivity window (not shared).
- No induced seismicity. Structure showed no response to M6.1 event (2041, epicenter ~60km).

## Logistics

- **Water**: Pacific desalination ~280km away, ~2,400m below. Watcher-powered pipeline (~23.5 MPa total head, 6 pump stations). CMC controls distribution (2041 concession; 2043 arbitration affirmed Chilean authority but left contract intact — stalemate). Ring 5: Syndicate-managed, ~35-40 L/person/day vs 180-220 in Rings 1-2. ALF targeted CMC water infra twice.
- **Food**: 12 hydroponic farms (Ring 4) + protein synthesis (Rings 2-3) = ~65% of calories; rest imported at premium. Quality follows ring gradient; Ring 5 food insecurity via Syndicate distribution.
- **Climate control**: Rings 1-2 full (~22°C, sea-level pressure). Ring 3 standard. Ring 4 basic (routine failures). Ring 5 none (76 kPa).
- **Transport**: Pedestrian Rings 3-5. Shuttles Rings 2-4. Executive vehicles Ring 1. No civilian vehicles Ring 0. External: Watcher-powered maglev to Antofagasta (~315km) + Santiago. Cargo rail. Airport ~4,200 daily ops. Ring 5: Syndicate motorcycle/cart networks only.

## Restrictions

- **Exclusion zone (100m)**: Military perimeter + automated sensors. Immediate detention for violations. No emergency exceptions without Armed Forces escort.
- **Commercial diving**: Requires Chilean Ministry of Science + Mining licenses, Accord registration, mandatory Guild membership (since 2039). Authorized to neuro-locked threshold (depth classified — recurring UN dispute). Below threshold: human specialist cert, Guild Black Team, 72-hour pre-auth, psych screening, continuous biometric logging. No robot-only ops below threshold.
- **Research access**: 15% international slots; allocation committee controlled by Chilean Ministry with military liaison. Heritage/US get ~40-45%; Crater Coalition/China below proportional share. 2-year max residency enforced. 3-year exclusivity routinely exceeded (~12% of active tracks over window, no penalties). Consciousness research below threshold requires enhanced psych eval + biometrics + Guild Red Team debrief.
- **Data**: Raw monitoring data classified (2037 national security decree); not disclosed to Accord observers despite Article IV. Horizonte data additionally under military intel protocol.
- **Emergency contact**: Heritage Foundation Article V protocols on standby below sub-level -100. 24/7 station in Ring 1 (Chilean Armed Forces + Heritage). All personnel below -100 must carry Heritage contact-event response kit. Never activated.
