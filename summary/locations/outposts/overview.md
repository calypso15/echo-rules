## Damage Classification

- **Destroyed (3)**: Complete vaporization, massive radioactive craters, no recoverable tech, permanent exclusion zones
- **Damaged (7)**: Partial functionality (heavily damaged to mostly intact), ongoing salvage, limited depth access, hazardous but accessible
- **Intact (2)**: Full functionality, deep exploration possible, active R&D, population centers

## Global Pattern

Sites positioned at: both poles (magnetic field access), major tectonic boundaries, deep ocean ridges, desert/mountain isolation points, equatorial + high-latitude distribution, Earth's energy grid integration points.

## Site Characteristics

- Surface structures: 1km hexagonal formations (where intact)
- Depth: Intact sites reach 140+ levels
- Energy output proportional to functionality
- Defensive systems showed varied effectiveness against strikes

## Current Impact

- **Geopolitics**: Intact sites dominate tech/economy; damaged sites cooperate on resources; crater coalition seeks reparations; access disputes ongoing
- **Science**: Intact = revolutionary discoveries; damaged = salvage innovation; destroyed = radiation studies
- **Environment**: Radiation spreading from destroyed sites; ecosystem changes at all locations; regional/global climate effects; elevated mutation rates near craters (no speciation yet)
- Each surviving site has developed unique governance, specialized research focus, economic systems, social hierarchies, and access protocols
