# ECHO-09: French Polynesia Outpost

## Location Data

- **Designation**: ECHO-09
- **Coordinates**: 16°24'25"S 145°36'05"W | Society Islands, near Tahiti
- **Status**: 100% operational, undamaged
- Standard 1km hexagonal structure, 140+ accessible sub-levels. Deepest human penetration: sub-level -119 (Feb 2046).

## Pre-Strike History

- France invoked Article 16 (emergency control) over French Polynesian waters at Contact. Territorial Assembly informed, not consulted.
- CNRS deployed ~400 researchers under CEA administration (same agency as Mururoa nuclear tests — parallel noted by Maʻohi observers).
- 50km maritime exclusion zone displaced ~3,200 Maʻohi fishing families (compensation widely criticized as inadequate).
- UN observers received limited access; reports subject to French intelligence review (protested internationally, denied by France).
- DGSE signals intelligence detachment monitored Watcher EM emissions.

## Post-Strike & Independence

- **Sept 9, 2036**: France did not target ECHO-09 — simply spared by its own host nation while authorizing the unauthorized Libya strike that killed 1M+.
- Zero damage. Resonance field fluctuated ~36 hours post-withdrawal (lagoon fluorescence intensified, coral calcification accelerated, two researchers developed early resonance syndrome), then stabilized at ~40% above pre-withdrawal baseline.
- France maintained cover story with more success here (Pacific isolation reduced leaks); local Polynesian staff were primary leak sources.
- **Sept 2037**: Independence referendum — 74% in favor, 89% turnout (highest in Polynesian history). Driven by convergent international interests: Chile needed an Outpost Alliance partner, Crater Coalition wanted to weaken France, Neutral Sciences Union preferred small independent host to discredited strike state.
- **Nov 2037**: Republic of Maohi Nui proclaimed. President: Hina Teariki (former Territorial Assembly president). 119 nations recognized within months.
- France protested, withdrew ambassador temporarily, re-established mission 3 months later. French nationals dropped from ~38% to ~4% of population. France retains 2040 bilateral friendship treaty (residency rights) but no governance role.

## Structure

### Materials
- Standard alien ceramic-metal composite on surface.
- **Unique**: Below waterline (sub-levels -8 to -18), Pacific oceanic basalt fused with alien composite at molecular level — designated **PB-Composite**. Higher thermal conductivity than pure alien composite. Contributes to ECHO-09's higher baseline power output vs ECHO-10 despite identical rated functionality. Fusion process not replicated. Evidence that Watcher construction performed deliberate site-specific adaptation (challenges standardized-pod deployment model).
- **Coral limestone deposits** (levels -10 to -22): Fused coral-composite zones exhibit anomalous acoustic dampening — selectively absorb resonance frequencies. Mechanism unknown. Potential resonance shielding applications. Findings shared via Article IV in 2046.
- Redistributed manganese nodule fields (~800m radius around sub-lagoon perimeter) with trace anomalous isotopic signatures from proximity to power systems. Licensed extraction by 3 Polynesian operators (~12-40 m₳/cycle).
- Self-generated cold bioluminescent interior lighting, no degradation since 2035.
- EM resonance field: ~320m from perimeter at waterline (smaller radius than ECHO-10 but higher intensity per unit area, attributed to oceanic thermal environment and PB-Composite).

## Current Governance

- **Republic of Polynesia**: Unicameral 87-member Assembly. Current President: Maeva Teriitaumihau (since 2043, re-elected 2046). Positions ECHO-09 as global research hub rather than industrial extraction (contrast with Chile/ECHO-10).
- **ECHO-09 Site Authority**: Controls access, commercial licenses, research allocation, Accord compliance. Single entry: **Gate Hiro** (named for Polynesian deep-sea deity), jointly staffed by Republic security + UN observers.
- **Highest Accord compliance ratings** of any ECHO site. All audit results published in full without classification exceptions (contrast with Chile, Australia).
- **Heritage Foundation**: Largest Pacific office, ~65 rotating researchers. "Cooperative and productive" relations with Site Authority.
- **Divers' Guild Pacific Chapter** (est. Oct 2038, second chapter after Nueva Esperanza): Converted copra warehouse 800m from Gate Hiro. 112 active members. Guild membership mandatory for all commercial diving (Polynesian law). Rescue teams: Red, Blue, Black, Gold. ~18-min avg callout (slightly longer than ECHO-10 due to maritime transit).
- **CMC**: Licensed commercial office, ~8% of active research licenses (vs dominant position at ECHO-10). Government resists preferential access applications.
- **Pure Earth**: Minimal presence. Republic's transparent governance + ecological restoration framing limits ideological reach. No recorded attacks. Sporadic protests from traditionalist Maʻohi religious groups only.

## Ecology

- **Within 320m resonance field**: Lagoon floor ecologically barren (coral can't survive, fish avoid). Fine mineral precipitate covers floor. Three documented episodes (2039, 2041, 2044) of large tuna/mahi-mahi schools circling structure for 12-48 hours then departing unharmed — behavior "inconsistent with known Pacific tuna schooling triggers." Unexplained.
- **Project Maro zone (200km radius)**: Coral cover recovered from ~11% (2040 post-bleaching low) to ~34% (early 2047). First actively managed large-scale marine restoration in human history.
- **Broader effects**: Measurable thermocline shallowing within 500km radius from thermal energy exhaust of lower-level power systems. Implications for fisheries and precipitation under investigation.
- **Maʻohi ecological knowledge** formally incorporated into monitoring since 2040 (joint Site Authority + Maʻohi Environmental Council). Produced observations predating formal scientific recording.

## Restrictions

- **Exclusion zone**: 320m from waterline perimeter, enforced by Coast Guard + automated buoys. Violations = criminal prosecution + Accord reporting.
- **Commercial diving**: Site Authority license + Accord registration + mandatory Guild membership required. Authorized surface to -87. Below -87 (neuro-locked): human specialist certification, Guild Black Team accompaniment, 48-hour pre-authorization, enhanced psych screening. No robot-only ops below -87.
- **Research access**: 15% international slots via independent merit review committee (3 rotating international seats). 2-year max residency. 3-year publication embargo (consistently applied without exceeding). Consciousness research below -88 requires psych evaluation + continuous biometric monitoring + Guild Red Team post-dive debrief.
- **Environmental**: 5km radius marine protection (no discharge). Bio sampling within 10km requires joint Maʻohi Environmental Council + Site Authority impact assessment.
- **Emergency contact**: Heritage Foundation Article V protocols on active standby below -100. 24/7 Emergency Contact Station (Site Authority + Heritage). All personnel below -100 must carry Heritage contact-event response kit. Station never operationally activated.
