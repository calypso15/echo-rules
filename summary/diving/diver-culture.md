# Diver Culture

## Post-2044 Split

Since robots replaced most human diving in 2044, culture split into two subcultures:
- **Robot operators** (80%): Control fleets remotely
- **Human specialists** (12%): Enter neuro-locked zones that respond only to living human neural activity; all bypass attempts (recorded EEG, neural simulators, BCI bridges) have failed
- **The Desperate** (6%): Can't afford robots, dive manually
- **The Called** (1.5%): Compulsion-driven, 80% never return
- **Hybrid teams** (0.5%): Robot-human coordination

## Hierarchy

**Operators**: Fleet Admirals ($100M+ assets) > Commanders > Operators > Pilots > Trainees

**Human Specialists**: Consciousness Masters > Elite Specialists > The Called > Contract Specialists > The Desperate

## Cultural Markers

- **Depth tattoos**: Operators mark fleet achievements; humans mark personal depth. Watcher ink reserved for human depths.
  - Operator ranks: Pilot (10+ runs), Commander (50+), Fleet Master (100+), Golden Operator (zero losses)
  - Human ranks: Mind Diver (consciousness zones), Void Walker (-81 to -100), The Transcended (-100+), Living Legend (still active)
- **Toasts**: Operators: "To the metal we've lost." Humans: "To those still deep." Mixed: "To profit and survival."
- **Slang**: "Tin soldiers" (robots), "Meat diver" (human), "Signal death" (lost robot control), "Neuro-locked" (human-only zone), "Fleet broke" (lost all robots)
- **Superstitions**: Lucky charms mandatory, never dive on anniversaries, Wednesday dives fail more, full moons increase danger

## Social Structure

- **Diver's Rest**: Cultural centers, segregated by type. Operator lounges have control stations, sim pods, success boards. Human bars have traditional atmosphere, medical support, memorial walls, "no robots" policy.
- **Guild membership**: Mandatory in Chilean and French Polynesian ECHO jurisdictions; optional elsewhere
- **Bonds**: Teams become family; trust is absolute; betrayal is the worst crime
- **Surface relationships**: Difficult to maintain; high divorce rate; polyamory common among divers

## The Calling

~20% of active divers affected to some degree (neurological basis unknown). Most resist full immersion. Recognized as occupational hazard. Governments in Santiago Accord jurisdictions attempt mandatory psych screening but enforcement is limited.

## Economics

- **Income**: Artifact sales (primary), information trading, guide services, equipment testing, rescue ops
- **Spending**: Equipment 50%+, medical 20%, pleasures 20%, savings rare
- **Retirement**: Rare; usually forced by injury. Teaching, consulting, or bar ownership.

## Health

- **Conditions**: Resonance syndrome, depth madness, anomalous radiation exposure, Temporal Dissociation Syndrome, pattern obsession
- **Life expectancy**: Active diver 5-7 years, deep specialist 2-3 years, The Called months
- **Death customs**: Body recovery if possible, memorial at the Rest, possessions to dive partner, name on the Wall

## Entertainment

- VR depth simulation, gambling (endemic), combat sports
- "Depth metal" (incorporates whispers), "Echo beats" (resonance rhythms)
- Survival guides, depth poetry, journals published posthumously

## Future Trajectory

As ruins activate: culture becoming religion, transformation acceptance growing, surface rejection increasing. Fundamental split: stay human/stay shallow vs. go deep/transcend.
