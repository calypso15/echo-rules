# Setting Overview

## Premise

Post-first-contact setting, 2047. Eleven years after alien species "the Watchers" withdrew from Earth. Humanity struggles to understand their technology and intentions.

## Synopsis

- **2035**: Watchers arrive, establish 12 outposts (ECHO-01 through ECHO-12) at specific geological/magnetic/energetic locations
- **18 months of near-silence**: Mysterious disappearances ("the Lost"), growing global panic
- **Sept 2036 (Echo Protocol)**: US, UK, France launch nuclear strikes against 3 sites. France also executes unauthorized 4th strike on Libya. Strikes destroy 3 outposts; energy cascade damages 7 more. 2 escape undamaged: Atacama (Chile) and Society Islands (French Polynesia). 2.8M casualties.
- **Watchers withdraw** from Earth and apparently the solar system
- **2036-2038**: Cover-up (attributed to "Watcher self-destruct"); collapses when Indonesian hackers leak submarine launch footage
- **2038**: Santiago Accord signed (147 nations)
- **2047 (present)**: Nations and corporations race to exploit ECHO sites. Divers explore ruins for governments, hypercorps, NGOs, or highest bidder. The wealthy deploy tech advantages; the desperate risk everything for a score. Everyone wonders if the Watchers will return.

## Core Themes

1. First Contact Protocol — understanding aliens with no common frame of reference
2. Scientific Discovery — comprehending advanced alien technology
3. Unknown Intentions — why the Watchers came and their continued silence
4. International Politics — competition/cooperation over alien tech
5. Preparedness — preparing for potential Watcher return

## Unanswered Questions

- What was the Watchers' purpose?
- Why these 12 specific locations?
- What was the purpose of abductions/releases?
- Will the Watchers return?
- Did the Watchers anticipate human aggression?

## Current Situation (2047)

- **Sites**: 2 intact, 7 damaged, 3 nuclear exclusion zones (Crater Coalition demands reparations)
- **Santiago Accord**: 45% technology distribution compliance; ~40% of artifact trade is black market
- **NATO**: Fractured; France diplomatically isolated after unauthorized Libya strike
- **China**: No ECHO site access; largest independent reverse-engineering program; defining geopolitical wildcard
- **Pure Earth**: Movement opposing all alien technology
- **Science**: Limited Watcher tech understanding; some material/medical/energy adaptations; ongoing exploration and decoding
- **Society**: New economy around alien tech; fractured public response across national/class/religious/generational lines; no shared factual baseline among global publics after 2036-2038 cover-up; religious institutions fracturing; new belief systems emerging
