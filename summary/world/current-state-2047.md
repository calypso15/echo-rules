# Current State - Earth 2047

## Global Overview

- **Population**: 8.4B (growth stalled by economic disruption, fertility decline, radiation mortality)
- **Casualties**: 2.8M from nuclear strikes; 3M Madagascar refugees; radiation deaths continuing
- **Politics**: Santiago Accord (147 nations) is primary governance framework; UN Security Council paralyzed
- **Tech**: Limited alien tech adaptation; hybrid devices emerging; traditional tech still dominant

## Power Structure

### 1. Outpost Alliance
- Chile (Atacama) + French Polynesia (independent since 2037 referendum after France's diplomatic isolation)
- 2 fully operational ECHO sites | 2.5M combined pop | Technological/economic supremacy

### 2. Damaged Site Consortium
- Antarctic Treaty Consortium, Mexico, India, USA (Wake Island), Australia, Portugal/Spain (Atlantic)
- Partially functional sites, salvage ops | 320K at sites | Cooperation through necessity
- **ECHO-02 (Antarctica)**: Most disputed governance — multilateral authority, 7 claimant states, UN oversight; Argentina/Chile/Norway strongest claims
- **ECHO-06 (India)**: China claims as South Tibet; brief 2035 border war; India retained with international backing; China maintains claim
- **ECHO-04/11 (Atlantic)**: Portugal/Spain claims rest on Santiago Accord nearest-capable-signatory provision, not UNCLOS EEZ

### 3. Crater Coalition
- Arctic nations, Libya, Madagascar diaspora
- 6M refugees, radioactive wastelands | Focus: reparations, cleanup, justice
- 2041 Crater Recovery Fund: US/UK/France contribute under protest; amounts widely criticized as inadequate

### 4. Pure Earth Movement
- Strongest in crater-affected regions | Political force opposing ECHO development

### 5. Neutral Sciences Union
- EU, Japan, Canada, Brazil, African Union | Mediating between blocs
- Uneasy coalition (EU includes France, Canada/Japan have US ties, AU priorities diverge)

### 6. China & Locked-Out Powers
- China, Indonesia, Nigeria, others without ECHO sites
- Largest independent reverse-engineering program | Demanding equal access via Santiago Accord
- Defining geopolitical wildcard

## Critical Issues

- **Site disparities**: Intact sites achieve breakthroughs; damaged sites struggle; craters contaminate
- **Three Craters**: North Pole (Arctic contamination), Libya (aquifer/Sahel desertification), Madagascar (Indian Ocean marine collapse, radioactive runoff)
- **Technology gap**: Widening exponentially; sharing disputes; 40% artifact trade is black market
- **The Lost**: Thousands taken/released 2035-2036, no memories, long-term effects unknown
- **Nuclear stigma**: US/UK/France suffered lasting credibility loss; Russia gained influence; no binding disarmament emerged

## Technology Integration

- **Adapted**: Basic power cells, some medical devices, communication improvements, material analysis
- **In development**: Energy weapons, shield tech, propulsion, data system decoding
- **Beyond reach**: Manufacturing, core OS, interstellar comms, spacecraft propulsion, energy production
- Note: "Quantum" label on Watcher tech is placeholder — phenomena extend beyond known quantum theory

## Environmental

- Three craters leaking radiation via ocean currents and windblown particles
- Cancer rates +300% near craters
- Crater heat islands disrupting weather; radioactive Libyan sandstorms contaminating North African rain
- **Intact site breakthroughs**: French Polynesia reversing local ocean acidification; Chile greening desert — not yet shared globally

## Economy

- New industries: Alien tech research, outpost services, defense contractors
- Declining: Traditional energy, obsolete tech, conventional weapons
- Wealth gap highest in history
