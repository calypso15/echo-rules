## Artifact Economy

### Categories & Values

2. Components: 1-100 m₳
3. Devices: 100 m₳ - 10 ₳
4. Data Cores: 10-1,000 ₳
5. Unique Items: Private BTC-only auction

- Black market = 40% of total artifact trade
- Information as valuable as artifacts

## Market History

- **Day 1**: Markets crash 40%
- **Month 1**: Trading suspended globally
- **Month 6**: Artifact speculation begins
- **Year 1**: 200% surge on Watcher tech
- **Sept 2036**: Nuclear strikes cause 25-35% secondary crash; cover story (Watcher self-destruct) manages narrative
- **Early 2038**: Indonesian hackers leak strike footage; third crash; capital flight to crypto
- **Years 2-3**: New economy emerges from compounded wreckage

## Winners & Losers

**Winners**: Early artifact investors, robot manufacturers, fleet operators, outpost nations, elite human specialists

**Losers**: Traditional manufacturers, human-only divers, crater nations, non-adaptive workers, pre-2044 diving companies

## Robot Economy (2044-2047)

- **Pre-robot era (2035-2044)**: 15,000+ human casualties, insurance crisis, limited capacity
- **Post-robot**: 80% operations robotic, ~99% reduction in human casualties, 300% efficiency increase
- Robot manufacturing: $200B/yr market, 5 major producers, 6-month waitlists
- **Jobs created**: 500K operators, 1M support staff, 200K technicians, 50K engineers, 10K elite specialists
- **Jobs destroyed**: 90% of human divers obsolete; generational unemployment; "the desperate class" created

## Corporate Dominance

- **CMC**: 35% of legal trade, price manipulation, vertical integration, monopolistic
- Other corps struggle for remainder via niche specialization and regional dominance

## Black Market

- 40% of artifact trade | Present in all cities | Violence regulated
- Services: Artifact fencing, enhancements, identity changes, information, assassination/protection
- Major players: Crimson Dawn Syndicate, corrupt officials, faction reps

## Global Economic Indicators (2047)

- GDP: +40% since 2035 (misleading — outpost nations +150-200%, crater nations -15 to -30%)
- Inflation: 300% traditional goods
- Unemployment: 25% traditional jobs
- Wealth gap: Highest in history

## Outpost vs Non-Outpost Divergence

- **Outpost nations**: Direct artifact access, crypto-friendly laws, attract global talent
- **Non-outpost**: Must buy artifacts at BTC premium, brain drain, traditional banks collapsing
- **China**: Enforces parallel financial controls via state-licensed exchanges with partial success
- **Smaller nations**: Capital controls largely fail; economic stagnation worst for those without site access or diplomatic leverage
- IMF/World Bank severely delegitimized; traditional sanctions degraded; economic warfare shifts to blockchain stake/validation power
