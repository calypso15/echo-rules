# Factions Overview

## Major Factions

### Celestial Mining Consortium (CMC)
- Motto: "Earth's Future, Today" | Founded 2037 | HQ: Ring 2, Nueva Esperanza
- CEO: Victoria Sterling | 45,000 employees | Market cap: $2.7T (2047)
- Apex corporate power; operations at intact + damaged sites; largest private diver force; transforms artifacts into profit

### The Heritage Foundation
- Motto: "Understanding, For Humanity" | Est. 2037 (UN Resolution 2847)
- Non-profit, board-governed | Leader: Dr. Noa Rosemann | ~750 members
- HQ: Geneva | Research: Santiago | Archive: Svalbard
- UN-backed research org prioritizing understanding over profit, preservation over exploitation

### Pure Earth
- Founded Sept 2037 | First attack: Jan 2038 (Chilean convoy)
- Decentralized terrorist/religious/political movement | Members: Unknown
- Seeks complete destruction of all Watcher tech and return to pre-Contact civilization
- Catalyst: The Lost | Growth: Exponential | Status: Major global threat

### The Divers' Guild
- Motto: "Ever Deeper" | Est. 2038 | ~3,000 active members
- Federated, chapter-run professional guild
- Union, mutual aid, and info network for freelance divers; born in Nueva Esperanza
