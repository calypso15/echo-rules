# The Divers' Guild

## Profile

- **Motto**: "Ever Deeper"
- **Founded**: 2038, in Nueva Esperanza bars/black markets
- **Type**: Federated, chapter-run professional guild
- **Members**: ~3,000 active
- **Role**: Union, mutual aid society, and information network for freelance divers worldwide

## Leadership (Nueva Esperanza Chapter)

- **Yuki Tanaka** - Guildmaster (deepest human return: -127)
- **Carlos "Ghost" Mendez** - Chief Scout
- **Dr. Amara Okafor** - Guild Medic
- **The Twins (Alex & Morgan)** - Tech Specialists

## Membership Tiers

1. Apprentice: 0-5 dives
2. Journeyman: 6-20
3. Master: 21-50
4. Elder: 50+
5. Legend: Extraordinary achievements

## Joining Requirements

- 3 successful dives, sponsor from current member, pass survival test, pay dues (work trade accepted), swear the Oath

## The Code

1. Share critical safety information
2. Rescue when possible
3. Respect claims (72-hour rule)
4. No corporate allegiance in Guild matters
5. What happens deep, stays deep

## Services

- **Information Exchange**: Real-time zone updates, hourly zone reports, real market artifact prices, crowd-sourced danger alerts, job listings
- **Equipment**: Gear library, emergency supplies, specialized tools, "no questions asked" policy
- **Rescue Teams**: Red (medical), Blue (extraction), Black (deep zone recovery), Gold (classified). 15-min avg response.
- **Training**: Apprentice orientation, survival workshops, equipment mastery, resonance development, deep psychology
- **Medical**: Subsidized treatment
- **Death benefits**: Family support fund

## The Diver's Rest (HQ)

24/7 neutral ground (enforced). Information trading post, emergency coordination, job board.
