# Pure Earth

## Profile

- Founded Sept 2037 | First attack: Jan 2038 (Chilean convoy)
- Decentralized, ideological terrorist/religious/political movement | Global | Members unknown
- Catalyst: The Lost | Status: Major global threat

## Leadership

- **The Prophet**: Supreme leader. Identity unknown. Communicates via edicts and digital avatar.
- **Inner Circle**:
  - Colonel Sarah Blackwood ("The Sword") - Military strategist
  - Dr. Marcus Webb ("The Seer") - Former Watcher researcher
  - Sister Catherine ("The Shepherd") - Religious coordinator
  - The Flame ("The Architect") - Bomb maker
  - The Voice - Media/propaganda

## Core Ideology

1. Watcher tech corrupts humanity (alien contamination)
2. Alien contact is a divine test of faith
3. Remain purely human (reject evolution/modification)
4. Return to simpler times (technology is evil)
5. Martyrdom in fighting corruption is glorious

### Religious Adaptations (not monolithic)
- **Judeo-Christian** (Western/Latin American): Watchers as false prophets, outposts as Towers of Babel, resonance as mark of the beast
- **Islamic** (North African/Middle Eastern, esp. Libyan diaspora): Watchers as jinn/fitna, tech as bid'ah, well-developed martyrdom doctrine
- **Hindu/syncretic** (Indian subcontinent): Watcher tech as maya corrupting dharma, outposts as asura constructions
- **Secular-nationalist** (East Asian/Eastern European): No supernatural framing; existential civilizational threat, cultural self-destruction
- Regional chapters blend with local traditions; theological contradictions create latent internal tension

## Organization

- Autonomous cells globally, 5-20 members each, limited inter-cell communication
- **Recruits from**: Families of Lost, failed divers, religious fundamentalists, nationalists, economically displaced
- Training camps: Remote locations, military tactics, explosives, indoctrination, suicide preparation

## Major Attacks

- 2039: Santiago Accord bombing (147 dead)
- 2041: Ny Fremtid researcher massacre (89 dead)
- 2043: Indonesian bio-lab destruction (234 dead)
- 2045: Australian convoy ambush (56 dead)

## Tactics

Suicide bombings, target bombings, artifact destruction, researcher/diver assassination, infrastructure sabotage, propaganda campaigns

## Resources

- **Funding**: Wealthy sympathizers, criminal enterprises, suspected state sponsors, artifact smuggling (ironic), donations
- **Equipment**: Conventional weapons, IEDs, crude EM disruptors (inconsistent, only affect human-adapted Watcher tech, no effect on intact systems), global safe houses
- **Intelligence**: Infiltrators in research, government, corporate, and diver communities

## Notable Converts

- Miguel Torres (famous diver turned bomber), Dr. Elizabeth Chen (geneticist, destroyed lab), General Tariq Farooq (leaked military secrets), The Burned Ones (divers damaged by tech)

## Impact

- **Political**: Growing voter base, candidates emerging, policy pressure, democracy threatened
- **Economic**: Billions in destruction, massive security costs, investment fleeing
- **Social**: Fear in outpost cities, research slowed, public opinion divided

## Public Opinion (regional, not global)

- Crater nations: 45-60% sympathetic to grievances
- Outpost nations: Under 5% sympathetic
- Non-outpost/non-crater: 15-25% sympathetic
- Rough global: ~20% sympathetic, ~35% opposed, ~45% no clear position
- Mass-casualty attacks harden outpost opposition while reinforcing martyrdom narratives in crater regions

## Faction Relations

- UN: Unfriendly | Heritage: Hostile | CMC: Unfriendly | Guild: Hostile (top target) | Synthesis Collective: Hostile (worst of the worst) | ALF: Hostile (spreads the contamination)

## Counter-Movement

Government infiltration, military ops, propaganda wars, legal crackdowns — limited success.
