# Minor Factions

## Artifact Liberation Front (ALF)
- Est. 2042 | Decentralized activist collective | No leadership | Global | Members unknown
- **Activities**: Artifact theft/redistribution, corporate sabotage, info leaking, black market disruption, public education
- Impact: Made basic Watcher tech available to those priced out; contributed to informal price softening at low end of artifact economy. No meaningful policy change.

## Crimson Dawn Syndicate
- Est. 2038 | Hierarchical criminal gang | Leader: The Red Queen | Ring 5, Nueva Esperanza | ~3,000 members
- **Activities**: Artifact theft/smuggling, counterfeit credentials, info brokering, black-market rescue/recovery, extractions
- Closest thing to law in Ring 5. Tolerated but watched by Nueva Esperanza security.

## The Found Initiative
- Est. 2038 | Non-profit, board-governed | Global | ~80 staff, ~400 volunteers
- **Focus**: Helping the Lost return home and reintegrate
- Operates in tension with intelligence agencies/health ministries that classify Lost as security/medical unknowns requiring state monitoring. 2041 Accord amendment gave limited standing. Governments routinely restrict case access or run parallel surveillance. Chronically underfunded.

## Ordo Duodecim (Order of the Twelve)
- Motto: "Voces Eorum Audimus" | Est. 2039 | Hierarchical religious, council-governed | High Council (twelve) | Global, major in Nueva Esperanza | ~50,000 members
- **Belief**: Watchers were divine messengers; repentance/meditation/sacrifice needed to prove worthiness for their return
- Growing movement attracting scientists, divers, and ordinary citizens seeking meaning.

## The Shield Brotherhood
- Est. unknown | Hierarchical cult | Leader: Brother Void | Territory/members unknown
- **Focus**: Opposition to and destruction of Watcher technology

## The Synthesis Collective
- Motto: "Unity through Evolution" | Est. 2044 | Flat corporation | Ring 4, Nueva Esperanza | ~500 members
- **Leaders**: Dr. Kenji Nakamura (radical neuroscientist), Maria Santos (former diver, "Maria the Untouchable")
- **Key figure**: Lilith — rumored first true human-Watcher consciousness fusion
- **The Choir**: Secretive group consciousness experiment
- **Activities**: Voluntary augmentation, extreme resonance enhancement, consciousness merging, Watcher system communication
- **Resources**: Unparalleled resonance understanding, resonance artifacts, possible AI communication, biological modifications
- Watched by authorities, feared by many, but producing results mainstream science can't ignore.
