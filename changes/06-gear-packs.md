# Gear Packs

To make things simpler, we will not use the Gear Packs from the Eclipse Phase book. Instead, at character creation, you choose one of the Campaign Packs, Profession Packs, or Faction Packs from below. All packs are 10 GP (even if they contain more than 10 GP worth of gear, which many do).

## Reskinning

In general, if an item can be reasonably reskinned in appropriate 2047 terms, it is allowed — and the reskin should be noted when the item is taken. Examples: hardware (H) sensory augmentations are external wearables (goggles, helmet rigs, handheld sensors); bioware/cyberware enhancements are Watcher-derived enhancement treatments (expensive, licensed, and conspicuous); smart animals are trained, lightly enhanced working animals from genehacker programs; the Flying Car is an eVTOL air taxi; Biomods are the Watcher-derived gene-therapy package standard in enhanced morphs — faster healing, disease resistance, shock resistance, and controllable contraception, but no aging immunity, limb regeneration, or 4-hour sleep. Items that cannot be reasonably reskinned are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the SRD gear tables.

- **Nanofabrication:** General Hive, Protean Swarm
- **Advanced Nanotech:** Healing Vat, Medichines, Disassembler Swarm, Saboteur Swarm, Pusher Swarm, Gardener Swarm
- **Combat Augmentations:** Adrenal Surge, Hardened Skeleton, Muscle Augmentation, Neurachem, Reflex Boosters, Respirocytes, Nanophages, Nanopump, Nanotox Glands, Energy Efficiency
- **Mental Augmentations:** Skillware, Skillsoft, Mind Amp, Multi-Tasking, Cybercortex
- **Social Augmentations:** Chameleon Skin (human), Endocrine Control, Neuromodulation, Sex Switch, Skinflex, Synthetic Mask, Synthetic Cover
- **Physical Augmentations:** Circadian Regulation, Cryogenic Protection (human), Cyberlimb Plus, Fractal Digits, Gas-Jet System (human), Modular Design (human), Nanotat ID Flux, Prehensile Feet, Retracting Limbs (human), Shape Adjusting (human), Skin Pocket, Skinlink, Thermogenic Protection (human), Utilimod, Vacuum Sealing (human)

Only common biomorphs are allowed in the default starting setting.
