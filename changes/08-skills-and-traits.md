# Skills & Traits

## Skills

The active skill list is unchanged except:

- **Psi → Resonance.** Same WIL-linked skill, renamed; see [Resonance](03-resonance.md). It also covers interfacing with Watcher devices, which makes it worth points even for support characters.
- **Free Fall** is niche: underwater operations at ECHO-04/11, parachuting, and not much else. Allowed, rarely required.
- **Pilot: Space** is unavailable. There is no crewed spaceflight to speak of in this campaign.
- **Flight** (the morph skill) has no valid user among allowed morphs. Skip it.
- **Medicine: Psychiatry** does the work psychosurgery used to do — it's how stress, trauma, and disorders get treated. Worth more than it is in the book.
- **Exotic Skill** examples for 2047: Disguise, Escape Artistry, Lockpicking (mechanical locks are back, baby), Sleight of Hand, Throwing Voice, Underwater Operations.

### Know Fields

Replace Eclipse Phase setting fields with 2047 equivalents. A non-exhaustive menu: Watcher Lore, Resonance Science, Xenolinguistics, Xenomaterials, Site Layouts (by site), Diver Culture, Santiago Accord Law, Artifact Markets, Crypto Markets, Corporate Politics, Geopolitics, Echo Protocol (the strikes), The Lost, Crater Zones, Radiation Medicine, Ring Politics, Crimson Dawn, Pure Earth, Ordo Duodecim, Echo Beats, Depth Metal.

### Languages

Real Earth languages. Campaign-relevant: Spanish (Nueva Esperanza's lingua franca), Tahitian/French (Gate Hiro), English, Mandarin, Hindi, Arabic, Malagasy.

## Traits

Everything not listed below works as written.

| Book Trait | Now | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Psi | **Resonance** | See [Resonance](03-resonance.md); Level 3 exists but is play-only |
| Psi Camouflage | **Resonance Camouflage** | You read as a lower tier than you are; also applies −10/level to instrumented Resonance screening. Beloved by people dodging registries |

### Adjusted

- **Exotic Morphology:** only valid for the Neo-Octopus, which carries it per its morph entry.
- **Age:** fully in play — there is no rejuvenation, and a 50-year-old diver is a legend or a cautionary tale.
- **Black Mark / Blocklisted Rep:** use the remapped networks in [Reputation & Money](05-reputation-and-money.md). A Black Mark with c-rep (license revoked) or x-rep (violated the Code) is a campaign-defining choice.
- **Allies / Contact / Patron / Enemy:** point them at Echo Protocol factions and NPCs. A Patron is most campaigns' mission source: a Guild chapter, a Heritage program lead, a CMC handler, a national program, or someone who won't say.
- **Resources:** as written, in m₳ — see [Reputation & Money](05-reputation-and-money.md). Purchases made with Resources are *owned* gear under [Persistent Gear](07-persistent-gear.md).
- **VR Vertigo:** kept; rough trait for an Operator, which is the point.
- **Hardening:** kept, and grimmer — in a world without backups, what exactly did you get used to?

### New Negative Trait

**Registered (Negative Ego Trait • Bonus 1/2)**
Your Resonance profile is in a registry. Level 1: a national or Accord database — screenings flag you, border crossings get longer, and recruiters know your tier before you walk in. Level 2: you're flagged as high-value — a state program, CMC talent acquisition, or worse actively tracks your movements. Most legally licensed Resonant divers have Level 1 whether they like it or not; taking the CP means it matters at your table.
