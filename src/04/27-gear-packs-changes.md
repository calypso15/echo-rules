# Gear Packs

The Eclipse Phase two-pack system (campaign pack + profession pack) is replaced. Each character selects **one pack** at character creation — from the Diving campaign pack, a faction pack (if eligible), or a profession pack. All packs are 10 GP.

Details and per-pack notes are integrated directly into [Gear Packs](27-gear-packs.md).

## What Changed

**Campaign packs:** The Firewall/Criminal and Gatecrashing packs are not used. The single **Diving** campaign pack covers the default starting loadout for all characters.

**Faction packs (new):** Two faction packs are available to characters who meet the rep or status threshold — **Diver's Guild (I)** and **CMC (I)**. These represent institutional backing and carry heavier or more specialized gear than a standard profession pack.

**Profession packs:** Thirteen packs carry over from the book. Each has minor substitutions — high-tech augmentations (Neurachem, Mind Amp, Muscle Augmentation, Medichines) are swapped for near-future equivalents; space-specific gear (Scout Missile, Pusher Swarm) is replaced with practical diving or field gear; licenses (L1/L2/L3) are added where the profession requires them. See the notes in [Gear Packs](27-gear-packs.md) for the exact swaps per pack.

**Removed:** Mindhacker. Psychosurgery does not exist.

**Added:** Operator. The most common profession in the diving economy. Pack contents are assembled with the GM from the Hacker, Techie, or Explorer packs as a base.
