# Echo Protocol — Overview

Echo Protocol uses the Eclipse Phase 2e rules engine, but it is not the Eclipse Phase setting. It is Earth, 2047, eleven years after humanity nuked the Watchers. There is no Fall, no TITANs, no solar-system diaspora, and — most importantly — no escaping death. This document lays out exactly which parts of the Eclipse Phase book apply, which are changed, and which are gone.

## The Five Golden Rules

When in doubt, resolve any rules question against these principles:

1. **One life.** There are no backups, no cortical stacks, no resleeving, no forks, no egocasting. If you die, you are dead. Any rule, item, or trait that assumes otherwise does not exist.
2. **One body.** Your morph is the body you were born with, shaped by genetics, training, and (if you can afford it) enhancement. You choose it once, at character creation, and you keep it.
3. **It's 2047.** Technology is near-future Earth plus a thin layer of Watcher-derived adaptations. If a rule assumes post-scarcity nanofabrication, ubiquitous brain implants, true AGI, or space infrastructure, it does not apply. See the [Prohibited Gear](../16/00-gear-changes.md#prohibited-gear) list.
4. **Psi is Resonance.** There is no exsurgent virus. The psi rules are repurposed wholesale to model the Resonance phenomenon — neurological compatibility with Watcher technology. See [Resonance](../14/00-psi-changes.md).
5. **Gear is property.** Nobody is printing you a new kit at the next habitat. What you buy, find, or steal is yours, and it persists between missions. See [Persistent Gear](../16/02-acquiring-gear-changes.md).

## The Campaign Frame

The default campaign: the PCs are a freelance dive team operating out of Nueva Esperanza (ECHO-10) or Gate Hiro (ECHO-09), taking contracts from sponsors — the Guild, CMC, Heritage, national programs, or shadier clients. Missions are dives into Watcher ruins or the surface-world jobs that orbit them (recovery, escort, theft, investigation). Between missions, downtime: heal, train, repair, sell, and answer for what you brought back.
