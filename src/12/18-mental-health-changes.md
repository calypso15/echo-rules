# Mental Health

Keep the stress, trauma, and disorder rules exactly as written — they are the right shape for a setting about staring into an alien abyss. Changes:

- **No psychosurgery.** Healing stress and disorders uses time, therapy (Medicine: Psychiatry or a professional NPC), motivation recovery, and medication (Comfurt and friends). This makes mental damage the slow-burning cost of the diving life, which is the point.
- **Setting-specific disorders.** Add these to the disorder list; they are the documented occupational diseases of divers (see [Resonance Phenomenon](../../summary/technology/resonance.md)):
  - **Resonance Syndrome** — neural inflammation and cognitive dysfunction from field overexposure; symptoms ease only away from sites and artifacts
  - **Whisper Psychosis** — you hear the whispers where there are none, and can no longer tell which are real
  - **Depth Addiction** — a compulsive need to be in the deep zones, with physical withdrawal
  - **Temporal Dissociation Syndrome** — you perceive multiple timestreams at once; nobody can tell you whether it's a breakdown or a perception
- **The Calling** is handled under [Resonance](../14/00-psi-changes.md#exposure-and-the-calling).
