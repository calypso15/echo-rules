# The Mesh

The Mesh chapter stays mostly playable, but the fiction changes: this is not the post-Fall wireless everything-net. It is the 2047 internet — recognizably descended from ours, partially decentralized by the crypto era, and **completely absent inside the ruins.**

## What Changed Since the Book

- **No implants.** Mesh inserts, cyberbrains, puppet socks, and access jacks do not exist (see Prohibited Gear). Everyone gets online through devices: phones/ectos, AR specs, gloves, terminals. Treat every character as using an ecto. The practical consequence: your access can be confiscated, jammed, dropped, or shot.
- **AR and VR exist** and work per the book. VR depth-simulation is a major entertainment and training industry; simulspace gear is allowed (it's in the gear packs).
- **XP and lifelogs are primitive.** Recorded sensory media exists as high-end entertainment and evidence, not a universal habit. No skimming someone's lifelog by default.
- **Surveillance is uneven, not ubiquitous.** Ring 1–2 of an outpost city is heavily monitored; Ring 5 and the world's back roads are not. The book's assumption that everything is recorded by someone applies only in secured zones.
- **Coverage is geographic.** No habitat-style total coverage. Remote sites, oceans, and crater exclusion zones are offline. Satellite links exist but are slow, expensive, and loggable.

## Muses and AI

- **Muses exist** but are device-hosted ALI assistant apps (Muse is in the gear packs) — capable secretaries, not co-pilots for your soul. A muse lives on your hardware: it can be stolen, subpoenaed, or destroyed with it. Restore-from-backup of a *muse* is normal data hygiene; there are no backups of *you*.
- **ALIs** run devices and bots per the book.
- **True AGI does not (officially) exist.** The 2048 Accord amendment on AGI legal status is still a proposal. PROMETHEUS — CMC's quantum-computing AI — is the setting's open question, not a player option. Infolife characters are not available.

## Hacking

Use the Mesh rules (devices, apps, authentication, intrusion, countermeasures, mesh combat) essentially as written, with these cuts:

- **No mindware hacking** (no cyberbrains to hack). Skip that section entirely.
- **Puppeting** applies to robots, vehicles, and devices — never to bodies.
- **Forking/infomorph rules** in the Mesh chapter don't apply.
- Hacking targets are 2047 systems: corporate networks, license registries, site-authority databases, robot fleets, the Guild job board, crypto exchanges. The Indonesian hackers who leaked the strike footage are this setting's patron saints of infosec.

## The Mesh and the Ruins (Important)

Resonance fields wreck conventional electronics and RF:

| Zone | Effect |
|---|---|
| Inside a site's exterior field (480m at ECHO-10, 320m at ECHO-09) | Wireless degraded: −10 to −30 to all wireless-dependent tests, range cut to a tenth. Hardened fiber-optic works normally. Unshielded electronics accumulate glitches over hours |
| Inside the structure (above neuro-lock) | **No wireless to the surface, period.** Comms and TacNet require line-of-sight laser links, physical tether, or relay chains (the Breadcrumb System in the gear packs exists for exactly this). Robot control runs through these relays — cut the chain and the fleet goes dumb ("signal death") |
| Deep zones / below neuro-lock | Electronics behave unreliably at GM discretion; relays fail without warning; recording devices return corrupted data. Below the lock, robots don't function at all. You are off the net in every sense |

Practical play implications:

- A dive team is **alone**. No calling for rescue without a relay chain; the Guild's 15-minute response clock starts when someone makes it back to a working relay.
- **Hacking inside the ruins** means physically jacking into your own team's tethered network or a recovered human device — not remote intrusion.
- Drones and operators stay topside or above the lock; below it, it's meat and fiber.

## Online Research

Works per the book against the surface net. The juiciest data — site telemetry, project archives, real artifact provenance — is classified, air-gapped, or sitting in a Watcher archive sixty levels down, which is why people hire dive teams instead of search engines.
