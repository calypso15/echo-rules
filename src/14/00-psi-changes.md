# Resonance

There is no exsurgent virus and no Watts-MacLeod strain. The entire psi chapter is repurposed to model **Resonance**: the documented neurological compatibility between certain humans and Watcher technology (see [Resonance Phenomenon](../../summary/technology/resonance.md)). The mechanics are kept nearly 1:1 so the book remains usable at the table — this document is mostly a translation layer plus one new subsystem (interfacing with Watcher devices).

## What Resonance Is (In Rules Terms)

Resonance is not magic and not a virus. Watcher systems establish a non-contact neural link with compatible humans. Attunements are your nervous system's adaptations to carrying that link; Projections are you deliberately pushing intent out through it. Exposure is how hard the link is currently gripping you — and the unsettling, well-documented fact is that the link sometimes pushes back ([Exposure and the Calling](#exposure-and-the-calling)).

## The Resonance Trait

Replaces the Psi trait (same CP costs, 2/4). Levels map to the Santiago Accord Technical Standards Board scale:

| Trait | Tier (Scale) | Abilities |
|---|---|---|
| *(none)* | Null (0) or Sensitive (1–20) | No sleights. Sensitives faintly perceive whispers near active systems (player's choice of flavor, no CP cost, no mechanical effect) |
| Level 1 | Attuned (21–40) | Attunements (psi-chi) only; may interface with simple Watcher devices |
| Level 2 | Synchronized (41–60) | Attunements and Projections (psi-gamma); complex device interfacing |
| Level 3 *(play only)* | Connected (61–80) | As Level 2; base Exposure 30; bi-directional deep interfacing without penalty. Cannot be bought at character creation — it is acquired through events in play, and it attracts the institutional attention you'd expect |

As with the Psi trait, taking Resonance requires taking a Manifestation and its associated negative trait (below) at no CP bonus. The Lost background **must** take this trait.

There is no in-play "infection vector," but Resonance can deepen: prolonged deep-zone exposure, the enhancement methods on the black market, or events the GM authors may raise your trait level (paying the CP difference in Rez, as usual) — always with strings attached.

## Manifestations

Replace the four Watts-MacLeod sub-strains. Use each sub-strain's influence-effect table and required negative trait exactly as printed; only the fiction changes. Prolonged coupling with Watcher systems reshapes minds in four recognized patterns:

- **The Architect** *(use Architect tables)* — The connection wants to *build* through you. Pattern obsession, compulsive arrangement, waking up to find diagrams you don't remember drawing. Common required trait: Mental Disorder (use **Resonance Syndrome** or OCD-pattern disorders).
- **The Feral** *(use Beast tables)* — Depth madness. The link bypasses your cortex and goes straight for the survival brain: frenzy, hypervigilance, the conviction that something is hunting you down there. Common required trait: Mental Disorder or Addiction (combat stims, Grin).
- **The Listener** *(use Haunter tables)* — The whispers never quite stop for you. Hallucination, paranoia, conversations with things that may or may not be there. Common required trait: Mental Disorder (**Whisper Psychosis**).
- **The Stranger** *(use Stranger tables)* — Personality overlay. Sometimes something else looks out through your eyes. Disproportionately common among the Lost. Common required trait: Mental Disorder (dissociative pattern) or **Depth Addiction**.

The "Playing the Infection" sidebar is excellent here — another player whispering as the connection is strongly encouraged.

## Exposure and the Calling

Use the Infection Rating rules unchanged under the new name: base Exposure = trait level × 10; Projections and pushes raise it; short recharges reduce it by 10; long recharges reset it to base; the 33+ and 66+ thresholds grant their printed benefits. Influence effects become **exposure effects** — moments when the connection exerts itself — rolled on your Manifestation's table.

Two flavor rules:

- **Exposure effects are evidence.** Mandatory psych screening for licensed divers exists precisely to catch them. A PC who visibly suffers exposure effects in front of the wrong people risks license review, grounding, or recruitment by people who collect high-Resonance individuals.
- **The Calling.** Whenever your Exposure is 66+, the GM may invoke the Calling: a standing temporary motivation, *+Go Deeper*, that follows the book's influence-effect motivation rules (satisfy it or take SV). At Resonance Level 3 the Calling is a permanent background pressure. Roughly 20% of working divers feel some version of this; 80% of those who fully surrender to it never come back.

## Using Sleights

Range, targeting, opposed tests, durations, pushing, full defense, and mental armor all work as printed, with these substitutions:

1. **Targeting people.** Resonance is machine-mediated, not telepathy. Projections targeting another person work normally against anyone with the Resonance trait, and against Sensitives at −10. Against Nulls, apply −30 — their nervous systems barely couple to the field. The book's "Psi vs. Psi" doubled ranges apply between two trait-holders.
2. **Field dependence.** Projections targeting other people require an ambient resonance field: anywhere inside a site's field (480m at ECHO-10, 320m at ECHO-09), anywhere in the ruins, or within Close range of an active Watcher artifact. Outside a field, Projections on others fail; Attunements and self-targeted sleights always work (you carry your own link).
3. **No synthmorphs/infomorphs to worry about.** Ignore those targeting exclusions; everyone is biological. Animals per the book. Robots and electronics cannot be targeted by sleights — but see interfacing, below, for Watcher systems.
4. **Sleight selection.** All psi-chi sleights are available as Attunements. For psi-gamma, the GM should reskin descriptions toward the setting's documented phenomena (emotional attunement, sensory transfer, knowledge transfer, presence sensing). Anything that reads as outright mind-control should be rare, deep-zone-flavored, and socially radioactive if discovered.

## Interfacing with Watcher Technology (New)

The reason Resonants matter. Activating or directing Watcher devices and systems is a **Resonance Test** (not a sleight):

| Task | Minimum Tier | Test | Exposure Mod |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perceive/parse whispers near an active system | Sensitive | Perceive or Resonance | — |
| Trivial response (doors, lighting, way-finding) | Attuned | None in calm conditions | — |
| Simple activation (handheld artifact, single function) | Attuned | Resonance | +1 |
| Complex control (multi-function device, directing a system) | Synchronized | Resonance, often a task action | +2 |
| Deep interface (bi-directional, site subsystems, archives) | Synchronized (−30) or Connected (no penalty) | Resonance task action | +4 |

- Every interface use adds its Exposure Mod and triggers an **Exposure Test**, exactly like using a Projection. Pushing works as printed (double Exposure Mod, 1d6 DV) for extra effect: faster, broader, or finer control.
- **Unfamiliar systems** impose −10 to −30 until studied. Each site's "voice" differs — ECHO-10 contact reads as ordered and directive, ECHO-09 as warmer.
- **Neuro-locked zones** (below −88 at ECHO-09; classified threshold at ECHO-10) respond only to living human neural activity. No robots, no recordings, no exceptions. This is why human specialists still exist — and why every sponsor wants a Resonant on the team.
- **Nulls and devices:** A Null can carry an already-active artifact and benefit from it, but cannot activate, direct, or shut one down.

## Resonance Measurement & Paperwork

For screenings, licenses, and registries, a PC's tested score falls in their tier's band (Attuned PCs test at 21–40, etc.). Falsified Resonance records — inflated and suppressed — are a thriving black-market service, and a perfectly good reason a character's papers don't match their abilities.
