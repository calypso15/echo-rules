# Gear — Echo Protocol

## Reskinning

If an item can be reasonably reskinned in appropriate 2047 terms, it is allowed — and the reskin should be noted when the item is taken. Examples: hardware (H) sensory augmentations are external wearables (goggles, helmet rigs, handheld sensors); bioware/cyberware enhancements are Watcher-derived enhancement treatments (expensive, licensed, and conspicuous); smart animals are trained, lightly enhanced working animals from genehacker programs; the Flying Car is an eVTOL air taxi; Biomods are the Watcher-derived gene-therapy package standard in enhanced morphs — faster healing, disease resistance, shock resistance, and controllable contraception, but no aging immunity, limb regeneration, or 4-hour sleep. Items that cannot be reasonably reskinned are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the SRD gear tables.

## Prohibited Gear

The following items either do not exist or are not available:

- **Ego Technology:** Cortical Stack, Cyberbrain, Access Jacks, Mesh Inserts, Puppet Sock (human), Ego Bridge, Emergency Farcaster, Ghostrider Module, Multiple Personalities, Remote Backup Link, Dead Switch, Copylock, Auto-Erase, Lockbox App, Psychosurgery App
- **Meshware:** App-Lock, E-Veil, Enhanced Security, Fault Tolerance, Persistence
- **Nanofabrication:** General Hive, Protean Swarm
- **Advanced Nanotech:** Healing Vat, Medichines, Disassembler Swarm, Saboteur Swarm, Pusher Swarm, Gardener Swarm
- **Combat Augmentations:** Adrenal Surge, Hardened Skeleton, Muscle Augmentation, Neurachem, Reflex Boosters, Respirocytes, Nanophages, Nanopump, Nanotox Glands, Energy Efficiency
- **Mental Augmentations:** Skillware, Skillsoft, Mind Amp, Multi-Tasking, Cybercortex
- **Social Augmentations:** Chameleon Skin (human), Endocrine Control, Neuromodulation, Sex Switch, Skinflex, Synthetic Mask, Synthetic Cover
- **Physical Augmentations:** Circadian Regulation, Cryogenic Protection (human), Cyberlimb Plus, Fractal Digits, Gas-Jet System (human), Modular Design (human), Nanotat ID Flux, Prehensile Feet, Retracting Limbs (human), Shape Adjusting (human), Skin Pocket, Skinlink, Thermogenic Protection (human), Utilimod, Vacuum Sealing (human)
